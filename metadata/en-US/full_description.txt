AisleSpy helps you see what’s really in the aisle—without tracking you.

Scan a barcode (or type it) for food and beauty products. AisleSpy looks up open data from Open Food Facts and Open Beauty Facts, then scores the product from 1 to 100 and lists problem ingredients with short explanations.

FEATURES
• Barcode scanner and manual entry
• Food and cosmetics in one app
• Clear 1–100 score (higher is better)
• Suspect ingredients with plain-language “why”
• Local scan history only—no accounts
• Modern Material interface
• Works without Google Play Services
• Free and open source

PRIVACY
AisleSpy does not create accounts or ship analytics SDKs. Scan history stays on your device. Looking up a product sends the barcode to Open Food Facts or Open Beauty Facts over HTTPS so the app can fetch public product data.

DATA
Product information comes from the Open Food Facts and Open Beauty Facts communities (Open Database License). Scoring methodology is documented in the app and source repository.

NOT MEDICAL ADVICE
Scores are informational tools for reading labels. They are not medical advice, allergen guarantees, or safety certifications. Always check the physical package.

Anti-Features: uses the network to look up products.
